## Overview

Oura's web presence is the visual translation of its hardware: **premium, calm, scientific, and Nordic.** The entire page is built on a warm ivory-cream canvas (`{colors.cream-100}` — #f7f1e8, "sandstone" in the codebase), not white, and the dominant "color" of the whole system is a single dark warm-brown ink (`{colors.primary}` — #4a4741, `sandstone-500`) that does triple duty as headline text, body text, and the fill of every primary CTA pill. There is no loud brand color. The only genuinely saturated accents are a **signal blue** (`{colors.accent-blue}` — #2a72de, used for the header "Shop Now" CTA and HSA/FSA buttons) and the muted **olive** (`{colors.olive-700}` — #5b6550) used for testimonial attributions. Everything else sits inside the cream-and-charcoal spectrum.

The type system is a deliberate **two-family editorial pairing**: **Editorial New** (Pangram Pangram's *PP Editorial New*, loaded only at ultralight 200 and light 300, with an ultralight italic) renders hero and campaign headlines at 48–110px with tight tracking (`-0.05em`), while **AkkuratLL** (Lineto's Akkurat, loaded at light 300 and regular 400) renders every structural heading, body paragraph, nav link, and button label. Headlines carry `font-weight: 300` — the system never uses heavy weights for hierarchy; it relies on size, spacing, and the serif/sans contrast instead. The brand's italic accent habit (`*your everyday*`, `*live healthier*`) is an Editorial New ultralight-italic emphasis inside otherwise sans copy.

The shape language is **soft and pill-dominant**: every button is fully rounded (`{rounded.full}`), photography clips at 8px (`{rounded.lg}`), testimonial and news cards use 8px plus a 1px hairline (`{colors.hairline}` — #d3d1ce), the newsletter input uses 12px (`{rounded.xl}`), and the two big "wrapped" moments — the hero band and the dark footer — are radiused on their lower edges (48px). Photography is warm, human, and natural: hands with rings, sleeping faces, morning light, household moments, always staged against cream or soft neutral tones with a slight film-grain quality. Motion is a calm blur-up reveal (5px blur fading to zero with opacity, triggered on scroll) — nothing bouncy.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Dark CTA, not a colorful one: `{colors.primary}` (#4a4741) fills every primary button with cream text (`{colors.on-primary}` — #f7f1e8). Secondary buttons invert the pair. This is the opposite of the saturated-accent systems (Rausch-red Airbnb, brand-purple Pinterest).
- Warm cream canvas: the page floor is `{colors.cream-100}` (#f7f1e8) end-to-end; there is no white body background. Section rhythm is achieved by alternating cream with deep charcoal bands (`{colors.surface-dark}` — #1c1b1a) rather than by white/off-white contrast.
- Two-family typography: Editorial New (ultralight/light serif) for display, AkkuratLL (light/regular grotesque) for everything else. Weights stay at 200–400 except bold 700 reserved for buttons, badges, and eyebrow labels.
- Pill buttons everywhere: 9999px radius on all CTAs, icon circles at 40/48px, and circular 44–48px arrow buttons on news/testimonial cards.
- Rounded-clip photography: images sit in `{rounded.lg}` (8px) frames on the cream canvas — no drop shadows on imagery except the hero band's `shadow-2xl`.
- Underlined tab strip: scenario tabs (Starting your day / Taking a walk / …) are text with a 3px bottom rule — active rule is `currentColor`, inactive is transparent with 70% opacity on the label; a 1px `{colors.divider}` (#cbcbcb) baseline runs beneath the whole row.
- Editorial film-motion: blur-up reveal (5px blur → 0) with opacity fade on scroll; no spring/elastic physics.
- Scandinavian/Nordic copy voice: "Subtle. Power.", "Built to blend in", "Rooted in wellness, not burnout", "FORM MEETS FUNCTION" — short, calm, confident, no exclamation marks.

### Brand & Accent
- **Ink / Primary** (`{colors.primary}` — #4a4741, token `sandstone-500`): the single load-bearing brand color. Doubles as primary CTA fill and as the default text color on the cream canvas. A warm dark brown-gray — reads as near-black but carries the same warmth as the cream.
- **Ink Hover** (`{colors.primary-hover}` — #1c1b1a, `gray-600`): the pointer-down / hover fill on primary pills. A true near-black.
- **On Primary** (`{colors.on-primary}` — #f7f1e8): cream text rendered on the dark CTA pills. The inverted-cream relationship is the brand's signature.
- **Signal Blue** (`{colors.accent-blue}` — #2a72de, `blue-100`): the only saturated accent. Used for the sticky header "Shop Now" pill (`{component.button-blue}`), the FSA/HSA eligible CTA on the home hero, and checkmark/connected states.
- **Signal Blue Hover** (`{colors.accent-blue-hover}` — #2056a6, `blue-200`): pressed state of the blue pill.
- **Olive** (`{colors.olive-700}` — #5b6550, `olive-600` — #5c6f5d): the muted sage used for testimonial author attributions ("Jussi L., Oura Member") and the small quote-leaf glyph. A quiet organic secondary tone, not decorative.

### Surface
- **Cream Canvas** (`{colors.cream-100}` — #f7f1e8, `sandstone-200`): the page floor for the home hero, body, and most sections. The whole site reads cream, not white.
- **Cream Light** (`{colors.cream-50}` — #fefaef, `sandstone-100`): the palest end of the scale, used for secondary-pill borders on responsive CTAs.
- **Sandstone Mid** (`{colors.sandstone-300}` — #efeae2; `{colors.sandstone-400}` — #efe6db; `{colors.sandstone-450}` — #e6ded3): progressive warm tints; the disabled/placeholder end (#e6ded3) doubles as `{colors.primary-disabled}`.
- **White** (`{colors.white}` — #ffffff): reserved for the testimonial cards (which float cream-on-cream and need a white plate), payment-method pills, and icon plates. Not the page floor.
- **Gray 100** (`{colors.gray-100}` — #f3f1f0): the PDP hero "Shop Now" button fill on light product photography.
- **Gray 150** (`{colors.gray-150}` — #e2e1da): circular icon plates behind stat tiles (`{component.stat-icon-circle}`).
- **Dark Surface** (`{colors.surface-dark}` — #1c1b1a, `gray-600`): the charcoal band behind the "In the News" section and the footer.
- **Dark Elevated** (`{colors.surface-dark-elevated}` — #3a3837): the lighter end of the footer's radial gradient, top-left.
- **Darkest** (`{colors.surface-darkest}` — #151619, `backdrop-600`): modal/backdrop tones used by overlays.

### Text

- **Gray 350** (`{colors.gray-350}` — #a8a5a0): soft secondary headings (e.g., the serif caption above news cards).
- **Gray 400** (`{colors.gray-400}` — #838280): muted on-light text; also the hover fill for secondary pills on cream.

### Semantic
- **Green** (`{colors.green-600}` — #55dc83, `green-pale` — #e2edd5): the app's "good score" green, used sparingly in marketing (e.g., check marks, readiness visuals). Not a web CTA color.
- **Red** (`{colors.red-600}` — #d22c15): error / destructive tone in the theme; not observed on marketing surfaces.
- **Blue** as above (`{colors.accent-blue}`) also serves as the focus/action semantic.

## Typography

### Font Family
The system loads exactly four font files:
- **AkkuratLL** (Lineto's Akkurat): `Regular` (400) and `Light` (300) — the entire sans UI stack. Used for structural headings, all body copy, nav, tabs, buttons, badges, footnotes.
- **Editorial New** (*PP Editorial New*): `Light` (300), `Ultralight` (200), and `Ultralight Italic` (200) — the display serif. Used for hero headlines, large campaign statements, and the italic emphasis words that appear throughout ("*your everyday*", "*live healthier*", "*women's health*").

Both are served via `@font-face` from `/assets/fonts/*.woff2` and wired into Tailwind as `--font-sans: "AkkuratLL"` and `--font-serif: "Editorial New"`.

| `{typography.caption}` | 12px | 400 | 1.25 | -0.025em | Stat tile captions ("r² compared to ECG²"), footnotes |
| `{typography.eyebrow}` | 12px | 700 | 1.25 | 0 (uppercase) | Badges: "FEATURED ARTICLE", "FSA or HSA eligible" |
| `{typography.section-eyebrow}` | 18px | 700 | 1.5 | 0 | "FORM MEETS FUNCTION" / "IN THE NEWS" over-eyebrows |
| `{typography.button-md}` | 16px | 700 | 1 | 0 | Standard pill CTA labels |
| `{typography.button-sm}` | 14px | 700 | 1 | 0 | Compact pills, outline buttons |
| `{typography.nav-link}` | 16px | 400 | 1.5 | 0 | Top nav links ("Shop", "Health Features", "Experience") |
| `{typography.tab-link}` | 14px | 400 | 1.5 | 0 | Scenario tabs (16px at lg) |
| `{typography.stat-number}` | 72px | 300 | 1 | -0.025em | PDP "99% Heart Rate Accuracy" figures |

### Principles
1. **Weight is restrained, size does the work.** The entire display tier runs at 300 (light); body at 400. Bold (700) is reserved for buttons, eyebrow badges, and the section-eyebrow ("FORM MEETS FUNCTION"). There is no 500/600 tier in active use — hierarchy jumps from 300 light → 400 regular → 700 bold.
2. **Serif for voice, sans for interface.** Editorial New appears only in hero statements, campaign pull-lines, and italic emphasis words; every functional label, nav item, button, and paragraph is Akkurat. The serif moment is the brand voice; the sans is the chrome.
3. **Two tracking personalities.** Display serif pulls tight at `-0.05em` (tracking-tighter); sans headings use `-0.025em` (tracking-tight); body is 0. Headline line-heights are 1.0–1.25 (tight), body is 1.5.
4. **Italic emphasis is a brand gesture.** Accent words inside headlines are rendered in Editorial New ultralight-italic ("Get the best sleep of *your life*", "Built for accuracy, designed for *your everyday*"), giving the copy a quiet, editorial cadence.

### Note on Font Substitutes
AkkuratLL is a commercial Lineto face. The closest open-source substitutes are **Inter** (for UI/body — matches Akkurat's grotesque proportions within ~2%) and **Libre Caslon Text** or **Cormorant Garamond** (for the Editorial New display role — both carry the light/ultralight serif range with usable italics). If substituting, keep weights to 300 for display and 400/700 for UI; preserve the -0.05em display tracking.

## Layout

### Spacing System
- **Base unit:** 4px (Tailwind default scale). Token steps observed: `{spacing.xxs}` 4px · `{spacing.xs}` 8px · `{spacing.sm}` 12px · `{spacing.md}` 16px · `{spacing.base}` 24px · `{spacing.lg}` 32px · `{spacing.xl}` 48px · `{spacing.xxl}` 64px.
- **Button padding:** standard pills use `12px 24px` (`py-3 px-6`); compact pills use `8px 16px` (`py-2 px-4`).
- **Card padding:** news cards 24px (`p-6`); testimonial cards `48px 40px` (`px-10 py-12`); image cards 24px inner.
- **Section rhythm:** the news band uses `32px → 64px` vertical padding (`py-8 lg:py-16`); the footer band `py-16` (64px); section heads carry `mt-8 / mt-12` (32/48px) offsets under CTA rows.
- **Gutters:** the site grid uses 24px side gutters at desktop, expanding to 64px at very wide viewports (see below).

### Grid & Container
- **`gridContainerV3`** is the site's 22-column grid: `--smallGutter: 24px`, `--largeGutter: 64px`, `--maxContent: 1440px`, column width `calc(1440px / 22)`.
  - Mobile: `[full-start] 24px [main-start] repeat(8, 1fr) [main-end] 24px [full-end]`.
  - ≥768px: 22 columns inside 24px side gutters.
  - ≥1568px: gutters widen to 64px (`[full-start] minmax(64px,1fr) [main-start] repeat(22, maxCol) [main-end] minmax(64px,1fr) [full-end]`).
- **Column usage:** sections typically span `col-start-main → col-end-main` (full content width) or narrower bands like `lg:col-start-3 lg:col-end-23`. Feature rows split `lg:col-start-main lg:col-end-14` (text) next to `lg:col-start-14 lg:col-end-main` (media).
- **Home scenario section:** a 2-column layout at lg — a left image rail with layered app-UI "mask" screenshots on cream, and a right column holding the underline tab strip plus the active feature panel; a bordered testimonial card sits in the right column.
- **Max content width:** ~1440px (`{--maxContent}`), capped by the 22-column grid.

### Whitespace Philosophy
The system is generously airy on the marketing bands (hero, feature rows, news) and deliberately layered in the scenario sections. Cream-on-cream sections are separated not by contrast but by whitespace, image masks, and the occasional dark band. The hero is full-viewport-height (`h-svh`) with centered type. Unlike Airbnb's dense card grids, Oura keeps one primary object per scroll — the site breathes like a magazine spread rather than a marketplace.

## Elevation & Depth

| Level | Treatment | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 0 — Flat | No shadow, no border | Most imagery, stat tiles, news cards, feature rows |
| 1 — Hairline border | 1px solid `{colors.hairline}` (#d3d1ce) | Testimonial cards, mobile section separators |
| 2 — Inset highlight | `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.06)` | Dark email-signup input |
| 3 — Hero shadow | `shadow-2xl` (0 25px 50px -12px rgba(0,0,0,0.25)) | The home hero band, which carries the only ambient shadow in the system |
| 4 — Frosted chrome | 1px `rgba(74,71,65,0.25)` border + `rgba(74,71,65,0.1)` fill + `backdrop-blur(2rem)` | The floating nav pill that fades in on scroll |

There is no progressive shadow ladder on cards. Depth comes from (a) the cream/charcoal color banding, (b) the 48px rounded bottom corners on the hero and footer that make them read as "sheets" sliding over the cream page, (c) layered photography — lifestyle image + app-UI screenshots composited in "mask" containers with `rounded-lg` clipping, and (d) the blur-up motion reveal. The only true drop shadow is the hero's `shadow-2xl`; the only glass effect is the scroll-triggered nav chrome.

## Responsive Behavior

### Breakpoints
| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Base (mobile) | < 768px | 8-column grid, 24px gutters; hero type at `{typography.display-sm}` (48px); scenario tabs stay horizontal (swipeable) with 16px padding; news cards ~75vw; primary CTAs go full-width (`max-md:w-full`); nav collapses to wordmark + cart/menu circles |
| md (tablet) | 768px | Grid widens to 22 columns; hero display steps to 72px; scenario tabs add 48px padding; responsive CTAs flip from dark pill to cream pill with border; payment row stacks |
| lg (desktop) | 1024px | Hero type at 96px; section heads reach `heading-6xl` (80px); scenario section becomes the 2-column feature layout; news band padding goes 32→64px; testimonial cards show |
| xl / xxl | 1280 / 1440px | Hero display caps at 110px (`xl:text-[6.875rem]`); content maxes at 1440px |
| super | 1568px+ | Grid gutters expand from 24px to 64px; extra padding tokens (`super:pt-[1000px]`) enable deep-scroll layouts |

### Touch Targets
- Primary CTAs ~48px height; compact pills ~40px with 16px horizontal padding.
- Icon circles 40px at mobile, growing to 48px at md (`size-10 md:size-12`) — at/above WCAG AA.
- Card arrow buttons 44–48px circular.
- Tab labels carry 12–20px vertical padding within a ~48px tap row.

### Collapsing Strategy
- **Nav:** mobile shows wordmark + cart circle + menu circle; the desktop link row and blue "Shop Now" CTA hide behind the menu sheet.
- **Hero:** full-viewport cream band with centered type at all sizes; photography re-stages below.
- **Scenario section:** desktop 2-column (image rail + tabbed panel) collapses to a stacked, full-bleed image + swipeable tab row on mobile.
- **News cards:** horizontal snap-scroll strip (`snap-start`, 75vw cards) at mobile → full-width grid at md.
- **CTAs:** product-page pills render dark-on-cream at mobile and flip to cream-with-border at md (a deliberate light/dark responsive inversion).
- **Grid:** column count drops 22 → 8 while the `main` column model stays stable; section banding (cream/charcoal) is preserved at every breakpoint.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Build the page on the cream canvas (`{colors.cream-100}` — #f7f1e8). White is a card surface, not the page floor.
- Let `{colors.primary}` (#4a4741) do double duty: headline text AND primary CTA fill. It is the brand's only "dark accent."
- Keep CTAs fully pill-shaped (`{rounded.full}`) with cream (`{colors.on-primary}`) text and a hover deep to `{colors.primary-hover}`.
- Use Editorial New (light/ultralight, tracking -0.05em) for hero and campaign statements only; use AkkuratLL for every functional label.
- Set headlines in weight 300 with `leading-none`–`1.25` and `tracking-tight`; reserve bold 700 for buttons, badges, and the "FORM MEETS FUNCTION"-style over-eyebrows.
- Clip photography at `{rounded.lg}` (8px) and stage app-UI screenshots in "mask" containers on cream — the layered screenshot + lifestyle image is a signature composition.
- Use the italic-emphasis gesture (`*your everyday*`) sparingly in serif ultralight italic.
- Punctuate the cream scroll with a charcoal band (`{colors.surface-dark}`) and radius the sheet edges at 48px (hero bottom, footer top) so bands read as layered sheets.
- Animate with the blur-up reveal (5px blur → 0, opacity fade). Nothing springy.

### Don't
- Don't introduce a saturated CTA color. The system's CTA is warm dark ink; the only saturated accent is `{colors.accent-blue}` for header/HSA actions, used sparingly.
- Don't set headlines in bold serif or heavy sans weights — 300 light is the display voice, 700 is for buttons/badges only.
- Don't float cards on shadows. There is no card-shadow ladder; separations use 1px `{colors.hairline}` borders and color banding.
- Don't render the footer light. The footer and news band are `{colors.surface-dark}` with cream/gray-muted text.
- Don't put buttons on sharp corners — every interactive element is a pill.
- Don't break the tracking discipline: display -0.05em, headings -0.025em, body 0.
- Don't use white as the body background, and don't use `{colors.accent-blue}` for links in running prose (links and CTAs stay in `{colors.primary}`).
